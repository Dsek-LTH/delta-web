Yes! Members of the D-guild can apply to be elected to work during the fair. As a committee member, you get the opportunity to be part of the work behind the fair and can get a wide variety of tasks:

<ul class="delta-bullets ml-[4ch]">
<li>Information desk</li>
<li>Company host</li>
<li>Night watch</li>
<li>Logistical responsibilities before and after the fair</li>
<li>Setting up electrical supply</li>
<li>Setting up network connection</li>
<li>Responsibility for wardrobe and lounge area</li>
</ul>

Applications are sent out via the D-guild app. So keep your eyes open as the fair approaches!

DELTA is arranged by a project group consisting of students from the four programs at the E-guild and D-guild at LTH, who in parallel with their studies prepare the fair and all our events. The goal is to have a fair where everyone feels welcome, and where all attendees feel satisfied with the fair. In addition, the fair would never be able to take place if it wasn’t for all the students that volunteer at the fair. If you have any questions regarding the project group or our volunteers you can contact our project leaders at general@delta.dsek.se . You can read about everyone in the project group under About us.

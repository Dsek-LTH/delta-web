AFTER-DELTA is the spectacular event that takes place about an hour after the fair ends. The goal of the event is to give representatives, students, and alumni the opportunity to network in a more relaxed environment.

The Delta project group has big plans for this event and high expectations. The ambition is to establish AFTER-DELTA as one of the best recurring opportunities for students, representatives, and alumni to meet and network.

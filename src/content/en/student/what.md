Delta is a career and future fair for D- and C-students, organized by the Delta project group, which is part of the business relations committee.
<br>
<br>
Delta is an enormously important opportunity for the guild to conduct its activities to the extent it does today. At the same time, the fair creates an important connection to the business community, which benefits both newly graduated students and the companies that participate in the fair.
<br>
<br>
The fair is therefore not just an event – it is an important part of the guild's future and relationship with the business world.

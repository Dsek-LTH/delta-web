Delta gives you much more than just job opportunities and new contacts. By talking to representatives from many different companies, startups, and non-profit organizations, you get the opportunity to develop a better understanding of what you actually want to do after your studies.

You can, among other things, get:

<ul class="delta-bullets ml-[4ch]">
<li> Insights into what you want – and don't want – to work with. </li>
<li> An idea of which companies and organizations work with what you're interested in. </li>
<li> An understanding of what it's actually like to work within an area you're curious about. </li>
<li> Insights into which paths lead to employment and what companies value in candidates. </li>
<li> Information about which companies offer thesis projects. </li>
<li> Information about who offers summer jobs and how to go about applying. </li>
</ul>

You don't need to be looking for a job for Delta to be valuable. Walking around, asking questions, and talking to different representatives can give you insights that are hard to get anywhere else.

As a D- or C-student, your presence is also one of the best ways to contribute to both your own and the guild's future.

**The more students who participate, the stronger Delta's importance becomes and the better the opportunities for future students.**
